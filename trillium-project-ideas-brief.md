# Technology Project Ideas for Trillium Flow Technologies

## Objective

Use software and AI to improve Trillium’s pump-engineering efficiency, customer service, and aftermarket revenue.

---

## Idea 1: PumpCare 360

### Situation

Cyclops² currently helps customers monitor pump condition and identify possible equipment problems.

### Opportunity

Monitoring a problem is only the first step. Customers still need to understand what action to take, schedule maintenance, and contact the correct service team.

### Proposal

Build a customer platform that converts pump alerts into:

- Clear problem explanations
- Recommended maintenance actions
- Maintenance tasks and service requests
- Pump documents and service history
- Energy, downtime, and cost-saving insights

### Expected Impact

- Reduce unplanned pump downtime
- Improve the customer experience
- Generate more service and spare-parts opportunities
- Create recurring digital-service revenue
- Strengthen long-term customer relationships

### Pilot

Connect 20–50 pumps from one customer to Cyclops² and measure how quickly alerts are converted into maintenance actions.

---

## Idea 2: RapidDesign

### Situation

Designing customized pumps requires several engineering iterations and computationally expensive CFD simulations.

Trillium is already researching AI models that can predict centrifugal-pump performance much faster.

### Opportunity

The AI research can be converted into a practical internal tool for everyday engineering and quotation work.

### Proposal

Build an internal application where engineers can:

- Enter customer operating requirements
- Find suitable previous pump designs
- Compare multiple design options quickly
- Predict pump performance using the existing AI models
- Select the best options for final CFD validation
- Automatically generate a technical proposal for approval

### Expected Impact

- Reduce design and quotation time
- Reduce repetitive CFD work
- Evaluate more alternatives before selecting a design
- Improve consistency across engineering teams
- Respond to customers faster
- Preserve engineering knowledge for future projects

### Pilot

Start with one centrifugal-pump family at Trillium Pumps Italy and compare the new process against previous projects.
